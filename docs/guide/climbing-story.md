# Climbing story

The **Climbing story** card turns your logbook into a short first-person blurb on the dashboard. By default it sits beside **Recent activities** near the top (Dreeve-style); Customize dashboard can move either card. It is interpretive copy from local stats — not a second editable logbook, and it never includes private ascent notes.

## What always appears

When you have a first logged day:

1. How long you have been climbing and how many active days.
2. Ascents, sends, onsights, routes and areas (plus outdoor/indoor when the account sync provides them).
3. Height climbed as Everest / El Capitan metaphors when height is known.
4. Ascents per active day.
5. Climber type and whether you lean onsight/flash or redpoint/work (same 55% majority rule as elsewhere).

## Optional lines (only when the signal is strong)

Up to **three** extra sentences are chosen in priority order. Near-ties and tiny samples are skipped so the card stays roughly 5–7 paragraphs.

| Signal               | Example                                                                                                    | Gate (approx.)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Routes you put up    | “I like to bolt sport routes that other people have climbed over 240 times. I've put up 18 first ascents.” | Style priority **Sport → Trad → Boulder**; need a clear put-up count. Community totals use **others** (paired ascent snapshots), never raw theCrag totals.                                                                                                                        |
| Home crag / wanderer | “I often climb at Arapiles.”                                                                               | Top crag ≥ ~40% of ascents, or many areas with no dominant crag.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Partners             | “I climb a lot with Alex.”                                                                                 | Enough ascents tagged with that partner.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Season               | “I'm a spring climber — Oct and Nov are my busy months.”                                                   | Clear seasonal majority of active days; spring/summer/autumn/winter follow your display locale's hemisphere (default `en-AU` is southern).                                                                                                                                        |
| Weather              | "I'm happy climbing in the cold: 30% of my outdoor days dipped to freezing or below."                      | Opt-in weather cache: at least **20** outdoor days with weather covering **half or more** of your outdoor days. One line at most: snow (3+ days), freezing (25%+), hot (15%+), rain (20%+), or a 30°C+ spread between your coldest and hottest day. See [Weather](/docs/weather). |
| Solo                 | “I've logged 5 free solos.”                                                                                | Free, lead or deep-water solo ticks above a small floor.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Open projects        | “I'm sitting on 3 open projects.”                                                                          | Auto-detected open projects.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Hardest band         | “Hardest sends sit around 24 / V6.”                                                                        | Hardest route and/or boulder send labels.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Quiet vs classic     | “I like quiet gems as much as the classics.”                                                               | Enough routes with cached popularity.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Travel               | “Climbed in 3 countries.”                                                                                  | Distinct countries on routes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Rock type            | Sandstone / granite blurb                                                                                  | Dominant tagged rock type.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

Putting up routes uses FA/FFA marks and setter credits from cached route history. Sport wording is “bolt sport routes”; trad is “put up trad routes”. Boulders only win when sport and trad are weak, because boulders are easier to put up.

## What it is not

- Not CPR, karma or a leaderboard flex.
- Not LLM-generated — templates only, so two installs with the same stats get the same wording.
- Notes stay owner-only on the logbook; they never appear here.
